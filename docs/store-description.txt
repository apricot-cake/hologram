=== SUMMARY (max 132 chars) ===

Save content you find on the web — image, text, creator, source and all — into a searchable library on your PC. Private by design.

=== DETAILED DESCRIPTION ===

No more "where did I see that?" — your social media library.

Hologram saves posts and artwork you find on the web—including original images and videos, text, author, date, engagement counts, and source URL—into a library on your own PC, where you can search, tag, and organize them.

YOUR DATA STAYS WITH YOU
- Everything is saved to a local folder you choose. No data is collected or sent to any server of ours.
- Images and metadata sit side by side as ordinary files. Open them in other tools, back them up, move the whole library — it is all yours.
- Your library stays with you even when a post or account is no longer available online.
- Save the whole library manually as a ZIP backup file, with an optional reminder after you save a chosen number of posts.

HOW TO SAVE
1. On supported sites, point at a post and press the save button in its top-left corner. Hologram saves all images and videos in the post together with its text, author, and source URL. The same button also saves text-only posts.
2. To save one image, choose Hologram from the browser's context menu. This also works for web images outside supported sites.
3. Import images and videos from your computer by dropping them into the desktop app or using the file picker. You can also paste an image from the clipboard.
4. Content saved in the browser appears in the desktop app automatically.

FIND IT AGAIN
- Search by text or username, with smart matching that forgives typos and kana variants
- Filter by platform, author, tag, in-text hashtag, date, engagement, and folder — combined freely
- Sort by newest, most likes, save date, and more
- Browse in a grid or list; in the grid, square thumbnails and show info are independent toggles. Posts with multiple images or videos open as one gallery

ORGANIZE
- Tags — filter to "No tags" and arrow from post to post to tag them one after another
- Folders for themes and favorites
- Bulk actions: select many posts and tag, file, group, or delete them at once

SUPPORTED PLATFORMS
X (Twitter) / Bluesky / pixiv

WHEN POST DETAILS CANNOT BE FETCHED
For some posts the platform returns no details—such as age-restricted or protected posts on X. When you press a post's save button, Hologram fills part of that gap from the text and author shown on the page. It tells you when details are still missing. A post that has already been deleted cannot be saved.

REQUIREMENTS
Saving and browsing require the Hologram desktop app for Windows (free and open source). Install it first, then add this extension.

PRIVACY
No data is collected or transmitted to any server of ours. To enrich metadata, read-only requests go directly from your browser to the platform you are viewing (X's syndication API, Bluesky, and pixiv — pixiv requests use your own pixiv session so logged-in-only works can be captured; see the privacy policy for details). Everything is saved locally to a folder you choose.
