Capture posts from X (Twitter), Bluesky, Misskey, Mastodon, and pixiv as JPEG images, and browse them in a companion desktop app.

Click the extension icon or press Alt+S, then click a post — it is saved to a folder you choose, together with its text, author, date, and engagement counts. The desktop app lets you search, filter (by platform, date, engagement, tags), tag posts, and export.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Click the extension icon or press Alt+S
2. Click the post you want to save
3. The post is saved to your chosen folder as a JPEG plus a small JSON metadata file
4. Open the desktop app to browse, search, and manage your captures

WHAT'S CAPTURED
- The post as a JPEG image
- Text, author (display name / handle), and post date
- Engagement counts at the time of capture
- Tags you add yourself

EXPORT & RESTORE
- Export your library to ZIP (images + metadata)
- Restore by importing an exported ZIP

PRIVACY
No data is collected or transmitted to any server of ours. To enrich metadata, read-only requests go directly from your browser to the platform being captured (X's syndication API, Bluesky, Misskey, Mastodon, and pixiv — pixiv requests use your own pixiv session so logged-in-only works can be captured; see the privacy policy for details). Everything is saved locally to a folder you choose.
